🛡️ Smart Home & Digital Safety Rules

  • 1. Keep Smart Codes Private: Never share passcodes for locks, gates, or garage doors with anyone online or on public forums.
  • 2. Two-Factor Authentication: Always enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) for your smart home accounts to prevent hacker access.
  • 3. Emergency Physical Override: Know where the physical keys to doors are kept inside the house in case of power or network outages.
  • 4. The "Check the Cameras" Rule: If you hear unusual noises outside, check your security cameras from a safe, central room first. Do not go outside.
  • 5. Verify Unexpected Contacts: If someone calls claiming to be a technician or service worker, verify their identity before responding.
  • 6. Emergency Siren Drills: Familiarize yourself with home alarms. Make sure children know what the sound means and what room to retreat to.
  • 7. Secure Wi-Fi Networks: Keep your home router secured with strong, complex passwords. Never use the default factory-set passwords.
  • 8. Trust Your Instincts: If you feel a smart home account has been compromised, lock the physical deadbolts and notify your parents immediately.
  • 9. Keep Software Updated: Regularly update smart home apps and devices. Updates often patch security vulnerabilities.
  • 10. Emergency Contacts: Keep emergency phone numbers written down physically in case your phone battery dies or the internet cuts out.
